RESEARCH TOOLKIT

Questions to ask before you invest.

Turn the concepts into a repeatable research process with practical questions and checklists.

Business Quality

  • What does the company actually sell?
  • How does it make money?
  • What drives customer demand?
  • Does it have an advantage?
  • Can the business scale?

Growth Potential

  • How large is the market?
  • Is the market expanding?
  • What drives future revenue?
  • Can margins improve?
  • What could accelerate growth?

Financial Health

  • Is revenue growing?
  • Are margins improving?
  • How much cash does it generate?
  • How strong is the balance sheet?
  • Is valuation reasonable?

Competitive Advantage

  • Why can't competitors copy it?
  • Does the brand matter?
  • Are switching costs meaningful?
  • Are network effects present?
  • Is the advantage durable?

Risk Analysis

  • What could go wrong?
  • What is the biggest weakness?
  • What could hurt growth?
  • What assumptions could fail?
  • What is the downside?

Investment Thesis

  • Why this company?
  • Why now?
  • What creates the upside?
  • What would change your mind?
  • What is your time horizon?
